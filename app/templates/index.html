About This Project

This project aims to leverage business intelligence tools and marketing analytics techniques to understand how customer sentiment and behavior evolve around major Apple product launches (e.g., iPhone, MacBook, Apple Watch). By combining structured sales data with unstructured social media data, this project provides actionable insights into:

  • Key drivers of customer satisfaction or dissatisfaction
  • Product features that generate the most engagement
  • Impact of marketing campaigns on sales performance
  • Regional analysis of customer preferences

Sentiment Trends

Measure daily sentiment from social and reviews with launch markers.

Funnels & Cohorts

Track awareness → purchase and retention by cohort and segment.

Campaign Lift

Evaluate incremental impact by channel, geo, and timing windows.
