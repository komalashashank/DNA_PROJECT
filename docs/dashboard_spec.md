Dashboard spec

Pages
- Overview: revenue, orders, avg sentiment, conversion rate; date and product filters
- Sentiment Trends: sentiment over time, launch markers, platform breakdown
- Funnel & Cohorts: awareness→purchase funnel, monthly cohort retention and CLV by sentiment quantile
- Campaign Lift: spend, impressions, CTR, conversion, incremental lift (pre/post or control)
- Product Detail: SKU-level trends, review themes, NPS

KPIs
- Revenue, Orders, AOV
- Avg Sentiment (vader compound), Post Volume
- Conversion Rate, CAC, ROAS
- Retention (n-month), CLV

Slices/Filters
- Product, Channel, Campaign, Country/Geo, Platform, Date range
